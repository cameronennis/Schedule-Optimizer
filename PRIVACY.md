# Privacy Policy — Schedule Optimizer

Schedule Optimizer is a desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, and the developer does not receive any of your data.

## Google data it uses

- **Calendar list and events (read-only):** used to find busy time when planning your tasks.
- **Calendars the app creates:** the app creates one calendar named "Optimizer" and writes plan blocks only to that calendar. It cannot modify your other calendars.

Google data is used only to build your schedule. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

## Where data is stored

- Tasks, preferences, and progress are stored in a local database file on your computer.
- Your Google sign-in token is stored in your operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager).

## Optional Claude features

Claude features are off by default. If you turn them on, the tasks, events, and plan details needed for your request are sent to Anthropic, either through Claude Desktop or through your own Anthropic API key, and are handled under Anthropic's privacy policy.

## Removing your data

Sign out from the app to revoke its Google access, or remove it at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored locally.
